How we ground ourselves.

We ground ourselves through small, everyday acts: preparing a meal, kneading dough, tending a garden, and sitting down to eat together. Earthbound makes space to notice our ingredients, follow the seasons, and feel connected to the work of our hands.

Our workshops bring these practices into daily life. Learn to cook with fresh ingredients, grow culinary herbs, and care for the soil. Alongside Star’s academic learning, these practical skills help us approach our days with curiosity, attention, and care.

Simple ways to feel rooted

Notice the scent of fresh herbs. Take time to prepare something from scratch. Learn where your food comes from. Share what you make. These are practices you can return to in your own kitchen and garden.

Carry it home

A meal you can make again. A plant you know how to tend. A little more connection to your everyday life.
